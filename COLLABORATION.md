# Collaboration: what we offer, where help is welcome

Updated 2026-11-02. Open an issue (see [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md)) or write to the owner.

## What we can share (LoL2)

- Decoded level-script exports for all 15 LoL2 levels (objects, groups, commands, event links, predicates).
- Annotated level maps with puzzle/NPC/item markers joined to the Prima and Brady guides.
- Item, spell, creature and shop tables collected from the printed guides.
- A DOSBox-based "oracle" method for measuring original behaviour (e.g. drowning time, memory writes).
- Our deviations ledger: every place the remake differs from the original, with its source.

## Open questions where original-game knowledge would help

1. **Huline villager speech in the Savage Jungle (L8):** besides the shaman's back-room lines (clips 225–237), which
   villagers speak, and what triggers them?
2. **Script opcode 18** (player/object move): its exact semantics. We use logged adapters at the Mage's Tower and
   the flood escape.
3. **Dracoid Cemetery:** the full receptacle → tomb-door mapping, and the lifting-table motion in the Ruins flood.
4. **Bacatta's storyline across Acts 1–3:** every meeting and the conditions that make him friendly or hostile.
   We restored his tavern farewell from CAN.WOM (a timer the port had missed).
5. **Hidden or unused content**, and the Draracle's Cave secrets.
6. **Good/evil (soul value):** we found the L20 cutoff (≥ 5 good / < 5 evil). Which other scenes or NPCs change
   with it?
7. **Original bugs:** any known bug not yet in our ledger, since we fix them all in the Classic edition.

## Ground rules

- No original game files, scans of the printed guides, or other copyrighted material in this repository.
- Findings are credited by name in the deviations ledger and the credits.
- Everyone keeps ownership of and control over their own project.
