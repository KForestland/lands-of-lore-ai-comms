# Shops and merchants, Acts 2–3

Grade HINTBOOK or BRADY on the sentence. No gold price was printed on the pages read for this pass. An exchange that names an item is not a gold price.

## Act 2

Herbalist, Savage Jungle. Prima p.89, and hintbook.txt 2991. The leftmost treehouse. She does not mind if Luther picks up items among her jars. That is not a sale, and no price is printed.

Shaman, same village. Prima p.89 and p.91. The first silverleaf is the quest. A later silverleaf is given to him and he gives the potion the book later calls the amaranth dose. No gold price.

## Printed "sells", before the River Bane

These are Huline village exchanges, earlier than Act 2. They are listed so they are not filed as Act 2 prices.
