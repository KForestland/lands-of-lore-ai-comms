## What the port fires today

E on a movable runs only when that movable owns a kind-4 record. The list is built at `act2_progression.gd:259-264`. The click is `use()` at `act2_progression.gd:1142-1144`: kind 4, `arg_c` 0, reach 120 (`:28`, aim at `:1128-1140`). Controls and props are not in that list. Exit E (`use_exit`, `:1146-1166`) runs only objects registered from `act2_travel.json`, plus the one L10 special `control:150` at `:227-243`. None of the globe-loop doors are that special.

A sword click is `aimed_struck()` at `act2_progression.gd:1232-1241`. The ray mask is 1. Mover bodies are layer 1. Control solids that exist use scenery layer 256 (`static_assemblies.gd:85` and `:170`). Scripted collision boxes are skipped in the default solid build (`static_assemblies.gd:99`), so a `scripted: true` cover has no body for the ray to hit. `_collect_struck()` (`:1195-1208`) does index every movable and control that owns a kind-9 record, and `_index_struck_bodies()` (`:1220-1230`) attaches mover bodies and `solid_bodies`. The ray still never uses layer 256.

Kind 4 mode 3 (`native_script_host.gd:740-755`, PROVEN compare): the argument object's dword +0x18 must equal the record dword +6. What supplies that argument natively stays UNKNOWN. The port's movable E sends `arg_c` 0 and does not send `arg_object_identity`.

Kind 9 mode is byte +9 masked with 7 (`native_script_host.gd:786-846`). Mode 4 and mode 1 are hosted. Mode 3 is not entered (`:845`). The sword supplies `word0` 0x080E, `word2` 0x0004, `dword_1e` = damage, `dword_26` = remaining durability (`act2_progression.gd:39-40` and `:1259-1260`).

Kind 3 has no matcher. `_match_object` falls through at `native_script_host.gd:783`. The commands of a kind-3 group are real. When that group is admitted is UNKNOWN.

Opcode 9 arm 0x15 enqueues that object's own kind-6 record with caller byte 0x14 (LIKELY, `:1546-1558`). A door whose kind-6 values are 20 and 2 does not match caller 0x14.

Opcode 2 arm 0x18 clears dword `[0x23348+0x90B]` and then calls callees that were not entered (LIKELY, `:1247-1252`). Arm 0x24, which sets player +0x228 bits 0x10 and 0x20, is a different arm and is not the arm in these charger and gargoyle groups.

Opcode 5 on a prop stores byte 4 at +0x30 when byte 5 is 0 (PROVEN store; the frame restart after it is the adapter). On a control or movable it stores +0x3D (PROVEN store; the follow-up loop is the adapter). Opcode 7 index 10 on a prop writes the low nibble of +0x37 from byte 5 (PROVEN, `:1473-1480`). A colour palette for that nibble was not read. Opcode 7 on a control is entered only for indexes 0 and 7 (LIKELY record-bit ORs, `:1501-1512`). Index 2 on a designator was not entered.

Opcode 3 kind 1 hands item identity u32 at +4 and byte 8 to a callee that was not entered (LIKELY grant, `:1633-1648`). Opcode 16 stores byte 4 at object +0x25 (PROVEN). Opcode 6 arm 1 is a list move on the object's 42-byte record (LIKELY, `:1873-1916`). Opcode 9 arm 2 releases the player (LIKELY, `:1527-1545`). Opcode 9 arm 0x0E sets +0x15 bit 0x80. Arm 0x08 clears +0x14 bit 0x80. Arm 0x0D clears +0x15 bit 0x80. Arm 0x12 clears +0x15 bit 0x40.

Predicate entry `00050000NN` with NN immediate is +0x25 == NN (op 0, left type 5, right immediate). Pred 206 is 0, pred 207 is 1, pred 208 is 2, pred 33 is 0, pred 213 through 221 are 7 through 15. Predicates 91 and 92 are operators 2 and 4 on the same left byte. Those two operators are UNKNOWN. Do not read them as "+0x25 == 5".
