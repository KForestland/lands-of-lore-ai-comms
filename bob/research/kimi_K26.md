  ## Problems

  1. **Patches are a stacked series, not independent.** AS02's `DEVIATIONS.md` hunk has AS01's temple row as context, AS03's hunk has AS01+AS02's rows as context, AS04's has AS01+AS02+AS03's. They only apply cleanly in the order AS01→AS02→AS03→AS04.
  2. **Deviation file-number collision.** Each of the four patches creates a *different* `docs/deviations/0309-row-2026-11-06-*.md` file (temple-approach, drowning-veil, temple-note, jungle-water-gaps). No filename clash, but four rows share the 0309 number.
  3. **AS01 scope creep.** The temple patch also adds ~30 unrelated draft deviation files (0310–0341: chamber of voices, elevator, fountain, atlas, ladders…) that have nothing to do with the fix.
  4. **Untyped `preload/load(...).new()` results.** AS02: `var overlay` in `liquid_drowning.gd` and `var drowning_overlay` in `cave_walkthrough.gd` are untyped (`drowning_overlay.gd` has no `class_name`; `Control` would do). AS04: `var skirts = preload(...).new()` in `jungle_walkthrough.gd` and `var scene = load(...).instantiate()` in the test are untyped.
  5. **AS02's jump test is not a jump.** The body is parked above the surface with a constant upward velocity for 10 ticks; a real jump returns to the water. The regression is covered, but "jump loop in mid-river" (the triage's own test description) is not what runs.
  6. **AS03 wording vs. triage.** The triage prescribes "south past the entry"; the patch prints "east of the entry" (the hintbook's phrase). The geometry supports east-from-entry, but the note also no longer tells the player *why* to go (the village stays closed until the visit) — worth a lead decision.
  7. **AS04 test never inspects its own captures.** It saves `as04_stream.png` / `as04_under.png` for human review and checks the node exists, but `build()` returning 0 (empty plan) would still leave the node present; only the data-level plan checks guard against that.
  8. **Not verified here:** AS01's claim that L8_SJ opcode 21 contains no snatch movie (so none plays) is taken from the patch's own research note; I did not independently decode the level script.
