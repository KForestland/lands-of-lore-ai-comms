# Act 2, silverleaf through the first temple shard, as a player

Grade HINTBOOK. The walk is Prima's order from the backlog titles for STORY-028 through STORY-053. Pages are those titles' Prima pages. Lines named below were read in `hintbook.txt` this pass. No Godot. STORY-028 through STORY-031 repeat the end of `act2_loop_duck.md` so this stretch stands alone. The hanging in the body room is STORY-054, so it is the next beat.

Brady's shore page is a different book. It is not used for this walk, and its names are not merged with Prima's.

## Walk

28. STORY-028. On the Savage Jungle lake shore, Dawn wants the bracers used to talk to the dead. Give them to her. Listen to what she says about magic that reflects, and take the next piece of curse-controlling magic. Prima p.90, 104. hintbook.txt 3027–3033 and 3862–3864.
29. STORY-029. On the way back to the cemetery, Baccata is hanging in a trap just south of the straightest path. Cut him down. He talks, then wanders off. Prima p.90, 117. hintbook.txt 3037–3045 and 3864–3865.
30. STORY-030. Speak with the Emperor's spirit. Without the bracers he cannot be understood, and nothing appears on the ground at first. Silverleaf then grows on the cemetery's dead trees. Prima p.90, 104. hintbook.txt 3046–3054 and 3865–3867.
31. STORY-031. Pick nine silverleaves and return to the Wild Village. Prima p.90, 104. hintbook.txt 3054–3056 and 3867.
32. STORY-032. Offer a silverleaf. The initiation fight with Daniel starts at once. It is not to the death. Prima p.91. hintbook.txt 3069–3074.
33. STORY-033. Offer the shaman another silverleaf. Keep the potion he gives. The book later calls that potion the amaranth dose. Prima p.91, 120. hintbook.txt 3075–3077 and 3119.
34. STORY-034. Tribe membership opens the clubhouse by the Huline Temple entrance. The guard opens the doors. The Ancients' Stones inside are what the book later says to spend on the citadel spawn. Prima p.93, 126. hintbook.txt 3138–3141 and 4174–4176.
35. STORY-035. With that potion and a silverleaf, jump the east river as Human, mix them, left-click the dose to activate it, and right-click it on the creature's upper chest so it sleeps. The same pages print both "Larkon" and "Larkan". Prima p.91. hintbook.txt 3120, 3146–3158, and 3880–3883. Brady's "Larkhon" is not this spelling.
36. STORY-036. You arrive in an icy gulch. Climb out to the northeast path and take the branch that is not the gorilla bridge. Prima p.120–122. hintbook.txt 3924–3927.
37. STORY-037. Stay off the glacier logs. Hug the right wall through the avalanche valley. From the ice ledge, make a running leap, as Human, to the cave mouth across the river. Prima p.121–122. hintbook.txt 3962–3989.
38. STORY-038. In the ice tunnel, hear Baccata out. Search Kenneth, take the picture, and throw it so it becomes his charm. Go on into the citadel when that special scene starts. Prima p.122. hintbook.txt 3993–4000.
39. STORY-039. Spark the globes. Destroy the light over the eggs so the basin drains. Take what it uncovers. Spark the elevator globe so it descends. Prima p.124. hintbook.txt 4086–4099.
40. STORY-040. On the ground floor, light the globes, including the ones that open Dawn's prison. After the Ruloi are dead, free the maidens with the switch on the upper left of the prison door. If any Ruloi still live, the maidens shut the door again. Prima p.125. hintbook.txt 4114–4136.
41. STORY-041. Free Dawn with the dreamstone. That use does not consume it. Prima p.125. hintbook.txt 4137–4139.
42. STORY-042. Climb the pink steps by the side ledges. Cross the bridge while it is visible. Fight the first Belial spawn, after the scene in that room finishes. The Ancients' Stones are the book's suggested magic for that fight. Prima p.126. hintbook.txt 4145–4180.
43. STORY-043. Do not take the dreamstone first. Put the sword you are using on the altar beside it. That makes the Dark Sword. Then take the dreamstone. Prima p.126–127. hintbook.txt 4185–4198.
44. STORY-044. Back in the hallway where you first appeared, set the dreamstone in the wall slot to open the way out, and take the dreamstone with you. Prima p.127. hintbook.txt 4203–4208.
45. STORY-045. From the ice opening over the river, drop onto a floe. At the river-level niche, spark the tripod so obsidian chain mail appears. Prima p.127. hintbook.txt 4209–4225.
46. STORY-046. Leave the floe by the glacier logs. Cross an ice bridge. As the Lizard, crawl the crevasse. Melt the blue bow out of the ice and grab it before it sinks. Prima p.128. hintbook.txt 4234–4245.
47. STORY-047. Climb the logs, go back to the gulch, and leave. The sleeper stays asleep until you are away. The book says you do not have to come back. Prima p.94, 128. hintbook.txt 4254–4259.
48. STORY-048. Jump the river passage as Human and take the tunnel or hallway into the Savage Jungle. Prima p.94, 129, 131. hintbook.txt 3173–3176 and 4263–4264.
49. STORY-049. The temple door is just south of that river crossing. Use the dreamstone to enter and take it back. Prima p.129, 131. hintbook.txt 4304–4309.
50. STORY-050. Pick up the dead Huline priest near the entrance. Fit him on the stone rectangle. Bring poison and aloe from the pew room, east then north. Prima p.132. hintbook.txt 4328–4344.
51. STORY-051. Put aloe in the west bowl and poison in the east bowl. Spark the body so it disappears. Prima p.133. hintbook.txt 4349–4367.
52. STORY-052. North of that poison room, take the green shard from the wooden post. The book calls this the easy one. Prima p.133. hintbook.txt 4370–4377.
53. STORY-053. Leave for the jungle tree with the raised platform. Spark the torches so stairs rise. Open the upper room with the shard, and bring the shard back to the temple. Prima p.92, 133. hintbook.txt 3104–3112 and 4353–4370.
