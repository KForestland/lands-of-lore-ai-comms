## Beat 40 - Summon Scroll Floor Trap (Hidden Shrine)

- **What the player does:** In the hidden shrine behind the boulder (right of the waterfall), Luther walks onto a platform holding a few items, including a Scroll of Summoning, and picks it up (lines 2309–2312).
- **What happens:** Taking the scroll drops the floor away behind him; when he jumps back across, rocky spikes protrude from the ground and the Gargoyles start spitting fireballs (lines 2312–2315).
- **Success outcome:** Two solutions — jump over the stone spikes, or use something like the Axe of the Long Arm to remove the spikes and escape (lines 2316–2318). Ultimate escape is through a small tunnel accessed via a small ledge to the right when the waterfall is behind Luther (lines 2323–2324).
- **Fail / edge outcome:** A possible bug can leave Luther stuck in Human form for some time; the book advises keeping back-ups, and saving/reloading seems to fix it (lines 2325–2327). (Entry note: the step-down into the area is too high to jump back up, so escape otherwise requires becoming the Lizard, lines 2305–2308.)

## Beat 43 - Password and the Thief (Optional Village Detour)

- **What the player does:** Optional "path of underhanded deceit": wander the village until you hear a password, then find the place for the password (lines 2337–2339). A door that won't open unless you've heard the password confirms you're on the right track — this is the thieves' guild (lines 5678–5681).
- **What happens:** This puts you in contact with a local thief and lets you get your hands on something nice without paying (lines 2339–2341).
- **Success outcome:** The deal: if you kill Kelsrick, the thief gives you the sword the armorer otherwise sells for the power orb; after killing Kelsrick you flip a switch and get access to a bunch of Ancients' Stones from the king, and can trade the power orb to fix the broken museum sword (lines 5682–5686). (Rumor: the fixed sword may be the best weapon in the game, especially turned into the darksword — "merely rumor and hard to substantiate," lines 5686–5689.)
- **Fail / skip outcome:** The diversion is not required; "the easier route is the honest one" (lines 2341–2342). Note: the hintbook places this in the Huline Village, not the Wild Village, and never uses the name "Confidante."

## Beat 49 - Rune Copies: Dawn, Then Julian

- **What the player does:** Make exactly two wax copies of the runes in the Hive Caves (lines 2809–2810; two bits of wax needed before entering, lines 2383–2385). As soon as you exit the Hive Caves you meet Dawn — give her a copy of the runes (lines 2392–2393; also 2701–2702). Then return to the Monastery, speak with Dawn in the library, and hand the second copy to Julian (lines 2393–2396).
- **What happens:** Julian provides very little information about Luther's curse but rewards you with a Power Orb (lines 2396–2397).
- **Success outcome:** Both copies delivered; Power Orb received (tradeable at the Armorer's Glen for the Great Sword Firestorm or the broken-sword repair, lines 2405–2408).
- **Fail / alternate outcome:** One copy would suffice for Julian, but you'd forfeit the Power Orb — the book notes Luther can even slay Brother Julian and steal the orb, "but that's so ... so ... so mean" (lines 2832–2835).

## Beat 50 - Boxes, Bookcase, Breach to the Dark Halls

- **What the player does:** After leaving Julian's office, enter the door to the left of the library entrance, chat briefly with the person there, then enter the storage area. Stack up some boxes next to the bookshelf, climb up, and attack the bookshelf with a spell or a weapon (lines 2398–2401).
- **What happens / success outcome:** You have breached an entrance into the Dark Halls; you return to this spot after leaving the Dark Halls to continue the Huline Jungle sequence (lines 2401–2404).
- **Fail outcome:** None stated — UNKNOWN.

## Beat 52 - Daniel's Gift (Kit'yara's Second Meeting)

- **What the player does:** Soon after leaving the Armorer, you meet her again (the hintbook never names her "Kit'yara" — she is just "her," the Armorer). Accept her gift and tuck it away (lines 2412–2414).
- **What happens:** The gift is intended for Daniel, her son; "it is the key to beginning the next major area" (lines 2413–2415). It is Daniel's gift from his mother — the Warblade ("Just give it to him, okay?", line 1670).
- **Success outcome:** Gift kept in inventory; later, showing Daniel's gift to the guard grants access (line 2982), and presenting the gift to Daniel is done by putting the sword on his chest (line 2904).
- **Fail outcome:** Handing it over wrongly matters — if Daniel's spectral form appears, you accidentally attacked him with the gift (lines 2915), and failing to pass the knife onto Daniel makes continuing "fruitless ... resulting in hours and hours and hours of pointless gameplay" (lines 2918–2919).
