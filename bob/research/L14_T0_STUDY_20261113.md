## What this does not ask anyone to build

No shift. The floors are already 3 to 20 apart, and the two places are separated by their ceilings and by having no neighbor word between them. The side file's "down-shift needed >= 1330" is the sky height of an open roof set against a span that still includes other open roofs. This note does not propose moving either place.
