The trigger crossing itself is walked and the goal is never teleported to, so it meets the stated criterion, but it is not a continuous walk from the save's position, and a static read cannot prove the straight 55-unit line is collision-free.

Established pattern in the suite, but the production deferred call for this exact trip is never exercised.
