## Beat 53 - Baccata's Bargain at the Bridge

- **What the player does:** On the way to the bridge across the major river to the north, find Baccata and walk up to him, then follow him to the bridge (lines 2416–2419).
- **What happens:** The player listens while Baccata negotiates for Luther's continued freedom (2418–2419), then a video plays of "all heck breaking loose on the Huline Jungle shore" (2419–2420).
- **Success outcome:** The Thom gate is broken, opening the way forward; the player follows the path to the cave and enters it to begin the Savage Jungle (2421–2422).
- **Fail outcome:** Skipping the meeting with Baccata means fighting a pack of Gladstone guards (2417–2418).

## Beat 59 - First Spider Attack

Note: the hintbook places this spider at a cavern lookout inside the Hive Caves approach, not at an explicitly named "jungle point."

- **What the player does:** Winding north, the player reaches a spot where Luther can peer into a cavern beyond; quick tapping footsteps are heard — a spider scurrying in the chamber (2598–2600).
- **What happens:** The fight is unavoidable — "A fight is inevitable, so save the game and crack those knuckles!" (2600–2601). The spider maneuvers well and dodges in and out of hand-to-hand range; the book advises keeping Luther's back to a wall and being patient (2602–2605, 2615–2619).
- **Success outcome:** After the spider is thwarted, Luther finds a pile of bones to the north (2623) and the abducted girl's mother in a nearby alcove, which sparks a brief set-piece video (2624–2626).
- **Fail outcome:** Beating the spider "may take a few tries" (2604–2605); if it flees and Luther chases it, he must not fall down the ravine (2619–2621).

## Beat 63 - The Flute at the Pit and the Eight-Stop Elevator

- **Prerequisite:** The player must already carry an iron flute and at least two bits of wax (2659–2661).
- **What the player does:** Return to the cavern where the spider was first found, take one of the two western openings down through narrow caves into a hallway that ends at an open pit, and use the flute (2663–2665).
- **What happens:** The flute summons the elevator: a flat floor section rises from the depths bearing a square tower with eight squares at its top; the top square is offset because the first floor — the current floor — is already selected (2665–2668).
- **Success outcome:** To go down, the player selects a floor between two and eight, counting downward (2668–2670).
- **Fail outcome:** Without the flute and wax, "you will be unable to continue with this level" and must go back for them (2660–2662).

## Beat 66 - Ride to the Top, Dawn Waiting

- **What the player does:** Return to the elevator and punch the highest block, then whistle again and leave the caves (2699–2700); equivalently, "Ride the elevator to the top" and head south (2818–2819).
- **What happens:** As soon as the player can "see the light" of the Huline Jungle, Dawn is seen waiting (2700–2701); the book confirms, "As soon as you exit the Hive Caves, you meet Dawn" (2392; also 2387).
- **Success outcome:** The player gives Dawn a copy of the runes when the conversation leads that way (2701–2702; 2392–2393).
- **Fail outcome:** Not stated for this exit; the only caution is that the Lizard can slip out through small top-level openings but must not transform mid-passage (2819–2822).
