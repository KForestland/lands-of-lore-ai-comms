# First Act 2 loop, as a player

Grade HINTBOOK. The walk is Prima's order from the backlog titles for STORY-001 through STORY-031. Pages are those titles' Prima pages. Lines named below were read in `hintbook.txt` this pass. No Godot. The loop ends when nine silverleaves are in hand and Luther is headed back to the Wild Village. STORY-032 is the fight that starts when a leaf is offered, so it is the next beat.

## Walk

1. STORY-001. Give Daniel his knife. When hoppers appear he drops it. Pick it up again. Prima p.87. That knife is what gets you into the village.
2. STORY-002. Go east. Watch the Huline Temple approach. A Belial-like creature snatches the old priest. Continue north to the village. Prima p.87.
3. STORY-003. A Huline guard spots you. Show Daniel's gift. He lets you into the Wild Village. Prima p.88. hintbook.txt 2979–2984.
4. STORY-004. Move through the village from right to left. Take items among the herbalist's jars. Click the shaman all the way through, and the doorway beside him. Hand Daniel the gift in his treehouse. Prima p.89. hintbook.txt 2986–2999.
5. STORY-005. The shaman wants silverleaf. None is growing. Go to the Dracoid Cemetery. Prima p.89. hintbook.txt 3000–3005.
6. STORY-006. At the winged statue, open the locked gate with the key the shaman already gave. Skip the zombies and the rat-like creatures. Prima p.98. hintbook.txt 3256–3260.
7. STORY-007. Tomb doors need a glass globe charged gold, blue, or white. Globes with swords in them are decoys. Gold doors come first. Prima p.97–99. hintbook.txt 3217–3228.
8. STORY-008. Enter the catacombs by the tomb just east of the entrance wall, or by the narrow stair. Light the braziers. Pick up loose globes. Prima p.99. hintbook.txt 3267–3295.
9. STORY-009. Charge one gold globe on the three-clawed charger to the northeast. The low fences are one-way the wrong way until the trip out. Prima p.99–100. hintbook.txt 3309–3319.
10. STORY-010. The white charger is in the ruins' Mage's Tower. If the cemetery teleporter works, walk into the winged statue. If it does not, use the pit in the Savage Jungle. Prima p.100. hintbook.txt 3330–3334.
11. STORY-011. The first ruins entry the book orders is that pit. Clear the opening Saar and stone monsters, go north, and take the first westward routes. Prima p.110, 112.
12. STORY-012. Spark the black orb to raise a bridge. The room past it has mirrors to the cemetery, the Savage Jungle, and another ruins spot. Do not take them yet. Prima p.112.
13. STORY-013. Enter the red-walled Mage's Tower, quench the green force field, charge two white globes upstairs, and kill the larger Saar. Prima p.112.
14. STORY-014. Spark the niche that holds the bracers for talking to the dead. Take the teleport room back to the cemetery. Prima p.113.
15. STORY-015. Open the tomb marked for a switch with a white globe. Flip the switch. As Human, tip the barrel so it rolls into the north tomb and spills oil. Prima p.101. hintbook.txt 3349–3362.
16. STORY-016. Set the oil off with dragon's blood or a level-three spark from a distance. A short spark is close enough to catch the blast. Prima p.101–102. hintbook.txt 3363–3409. The bottle itself is picked up in the museum, hintbook.txt 2113–2114, before this loop.
17. STORY-017. Charge at least two blue globes at the northern charger. A third reaches the Valkyrie crossbow, which the book does not require. Leave only by the stairs used on the way in, and by the low-fence jumps. Prima p.103. hintbook.txt 3418–3426.
18. STORY-018. Open the priest's tomb with a blue globe. Take the urn he gives. Go burn the body. Prima p.103.
19. STORY-019. Upstairs in the building marked for the priest's errand, break the north rail, follow the west bank, cross the bridge, ignore the pool room, and take the tunnel's east turn. Prima p.113.
20. STORY-020. Drag the body onto the altar, spark both orbs, click the urn on the ash, put the urn away, and return to the cemetery the way you came. Prima p.114.
21. STORY-021. The priest gives an ankh. Set it in the slab by his tomb. That opens the Emperor's tomb. Prima p.103–104.
22. STORY-022. Put a gold globe, a white globe, and a blue globe into the matching boxes in that order. Take the Emperor's remains. Prima p.104.
23. STORY-023. The entrance teleporter works. Take it to the ruins. Prima p.104.
24. STORY-024. Before the remains are used, the eastern courtyard alcove only makes Luther ask a question. With the remains, put them in the big bowl on the left. Prima p.115.
25. STORY-025. Follow the statue that walks off. Stay Human with dampen. The worm will not tolerate being knocked out of the room. Prima p.115–116. hintbook.txt 3810–3819.
26. STORY-026. Outrun the rising water. Ride the lifting table into the side tunnel. Leave on the grate at the precipice. Prima p.116. hintbook.txt 3823–3845.
27. STORY-027. The wet exit wakes you on the Savage Jungle lake shore. Dawn is there. The ruins then fill and stay shut. Prima p.89, 106, 117. hintbook.txt 3021–3023.
28. STORY-028. Give Dawn those bracers. She talks about magic that reflects and gives the next piece of curse-controlling magic. Prima p.90, 104. hintbook.txt 3027–3033.
29. STORY-029. Cut Baccata out of a trap just south of the straightest path back to the cemetery. Prima p.90, 117. hintbook.txt 3037–3045.
30. STORY-030. At the Emperor's tomb, without the bracers, he cannot be understood. Silverleaf then grows on the cemetery's dead trees. Prima p.90, 104. hintbook.txt 3046–3054.
31. STORY-031. Pick nine silverleaves and return to the Wild Village. Prima p.90, 104. hintbook.txt 3054–3056.

Dampen is already required by STORY-025. The monastery gift is before this loop. Dragon's blood is the museum pickup above. Hopper tunnels and the lakeside fish are in the tour after STORY-033, hintbook.txt 3077–3098, so they are outside this return.
