A walk-past is a step the original starts by entering a place, while the port waits for E, so a player can stroll through and the chain never arms. Click, item, dialog, and spark steps can be skipped by leaving the hotspot. That skip matches the book, so those rows are not the temple pattern.

STORY-001 through STORY-006 are outside this table. The first temple visit is STORY-002. AS01 already commits it on approach. AU03 is the priest picture for that visit.
