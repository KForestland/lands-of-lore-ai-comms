# LoL3 bugs and patches

Read-only. No patch file, disc image, or installer was downloaded. Classic's rule, from the planning note, is that every original bug is fixed and written down. The Godot host still has no earned route, so none of these rows is implemented. A row says what the original does and what Classic aims at. Cut content is not turned into a feature.

Sources: MobyGames patches, https://www.mobygames.com/game/849/lands-of-lore-iii/patches/. MobyGames trivia, https://www.mobygames.com/game/849/lands-of-lore-iii/trivia/. Bolle 2.04 bugs section, https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/197755-lands-of-lore-iii/faqs/2527. Fandom bug stub, https://lands-of-lore.fandom.com/wiki/Bugs. GOG store, https://www.gog.com/game/lands_of_lore_3. Riccardi's descriptions on https://archive.org/details/lands-of-lore-3-part-1.

## Official patches

MobyGames dates v1.06J to 7 May 1999 and v1.07a to 9 May 1999. The long fix list is printed on the v1.07a row. What 1.06J changed by itself is not on that row. A French paste of the same list on jeuxvideo.com matches the English bullets. It is the same note, not a second patch.

Classic aims at the patched result for each bullet:

- Direct3D initialization crashes, and card selection when two Direct3D cards are present.
- Lockups on cards that use paletted textures. Transparent textures drawing dark grey, streaked, or with a rectangular outline. Blending mode. Translucent textures.
- Cache file rebuilt after a bad shutdown. Command-line switches for some cards.
- Crash with Active Desktop. Crash on a CD swap. Sound lockup under Windows NT. Random crash after a cinematic. Alt-Tab crash.
- Several flash pots picked up together. Conversations at minimum volume. Items turning invisible. Pyra pods with no visible effect.
- Warrior and Wizard quests that did not start.
- Infinite experience in the Frozen Wastes.
- Quests that stay uncrossed after completion. Bolle still sees this after the patch. That leftover stays its own row.
- A second shard from the White Tower after the portal is closed.
- Mauri floating.
- Slow drawing while items move down the inventory.
- A secret room in Lower Gladstone that could not be left.
- Prism sword with no sound.
- Picking up the sigil or the seal teleports Copper to the wrong place and sticks him.
- Attacking the White Tower case after the hilt is already taken crashes.
- New feature kept: right-click the journal in the world and read the current conversation plus the last 25.

## Still present after the patch

Bolle numbers these. His bugs section is explicitly after the latest patch. His items 6 and 7 sit between Victoria and the elevator. Their headings were not in the extracts copied here, so they are not given titles.

- Bolle 1. Draracle's Lair, level 3. A wall pulls Copper in. He walks away backwards, one step at a time. He calls it an engine mistake. Classic: that pull does not happen.
- Bolle 2. Rhynn, met in the Talamari library, does not recognise Copper later in Upper Gladstone. Classic: the second meeting keeps the first.
- Bolle 3. The Blue Deer Inn keeper sometimes never asks for the room money, so the stored things stay unreachable. Classic: the debt can be paid and the room opens. A GOG review (Draugusto, 8 February 2021, on the store page) says the room's contents vanished after the second mirror shard, because the town changed and there is no other stash. That report stays its own sentence beside the innkeeper bug. Classic: stored room items survive the town change.
- Bolle 4. Frozen Wastes. The blue gem cannot be taken if the other gems were taken first, or if every mutant chicken is dead. Classic: the blue gem remains until it is taken.
- Bolle 5. After the second portal and the audience with the king, Victoria in the keep sells once. A later visit lets Copper pick goods up and then will not take payment or a theft. Classic: a later visit still sells.
- Bolle 6 and 7. Headings unread. They stay open.
- Woods, the sentence on that page immediately before Bolle 8. The woods can no longer be entered. His only recovery is the latest Gladstone Woods save. Classic: the woods stay enterable on the story beat where his page loses them.
- Bolle 8. Volcano elevator, Finch Heart of Sunrise. The dragon-claw urn goes up through Copper and leaves him below. His player remedies are to skip the quest, to reload from another area so the elevator loads again, or to reinstall and patch. Classic: the urn carries him back up. The quest stays completable.
- Bolle 9. Solved quests often remain in the journal. The patch names the same fault. He still sees it. Classic: a solved quest is crossed out.
- Bolle 10. The Finch five-herb quest can be crossed out and then offer no next quest. Classic: the next Finch quest is offered.
- Sioned. Bolle frees her with the dreamshard. Dawn does not pay and Sioned does not mention it. The fandom stub says Rescuing Sioned cannot be completed as intended because she never returns to the guild, and that there is no known fix. Both sentences stay. Classic: the return and the reward happen.
- Orcish Retribution. The fandom stub says Leina accepts the quest and does not give the item, and that there is no known fix. Classic: the reward item is given.
- The Perfumed Scarf, Mauri, and Solan. The fandom stub says the side quest does not start, and that there is no known fix. Riccardi's clip description says the scarf is on Samuel in the keep or on the Geldenberry man. Those sentences stay unmerged. Classic: the quest starts. The item's holder stays unread until a retail pass.
- A sewer grate on the left of Gladstone Keep. The fandom stub says it stays shut even when lockpicking is high enough. The page spells Gladestone. Classic: the grate opens at the skill the game asks for.
- Scavenger eyes. Bolle's journal says a right-click throws them. Copper does not throw them. Classic: the throw matches the journal, or the journal does not offer a throw. Which of those two the retail text intended stays UNKNOWN.
- MobyGames trivia: after the patch, bugs remain that can stop play. No titles. A player review on the same entry says finished quests are not always crossed out, graphics glitch, and the game sometimes freezes. The quest sentence is Bolle 9. The freeze and the glitch have no scene. Classic: a freeze that the review leaves unnamed is still a fix once a scene is named. TackProductions part 4 is titled as a game-breaking bug. The title does not name it. Cause stays UNKNOWN. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xydujDWTiY4

## GOG notes

The store page has no strategy text and no patch list. GOG's release date on MobyGames is 23 August 2012. gogdb product 1207659094 lists an English installer, a manual, and a reference card. No changelog text was on that page.

Riccardi, archive.org description of the part-1 clips, in his words: the Mauri money-stick quest works on the CD Windows version and, as far as he knows, does not work on GOG. A warriors-guild shop trick that made stock free is one he is not sure still works on GOG. He names missing spells and items, including Bracers of Valor, the Eye of Golum, Fay, Humuculous, and Shadow, and he says Shala from Guardians of Destiny does not appear, with a skeleton key supposedly in her shop. He also says some CD dialogue is absent. These stay his description. They are not a decoded CD-versus-GOG diff. Classic does not add a shop or a spell from this description alone. A later retail read can promote a row.

No fan binary patch was fetched. Riccardi asks for a 1.08. That number is his request. It is not a Westwood release on the MobyGames list. The old landsoflore.de mirror's description says the March 2023 site held patch files. They were not downloaded.
