## Problems

1. **`docs/DEVIATIONS.md` will conflict.** The patch's base blob is fd6db6b's tail; 0603225 appended six rows (0362, 0366–0370) and r19-bk seven more (0371–0377) at the same anchor. `git am -3` produces an add-add conflict; plain `git apply` would silently insert rows 0363–0365 *before* row 0362, scrambling the ledger order. The patch should be rebased onto post-r19-bk r19-work, or the row placement fixed by hand during merge.

2. **Fresh placement inside the L17 overlap can capture the wrong layer.** `_collision_region_at`'s fallback `progression.region_at` picks the containing region with the floor nearest the body origin y (act2_progression.gd:317–329), and `current_region` is not saved/restored (`var current_region := -1`, act2_progression.gd:37; no save field). A save taken while standing in the throat (inside both 562 and 1236 polygons) reloads with no sticky region: origin y≈32 favors 1236's floor 12 (dy 20) over 562's floor 0 (dy 32), so the 8-hop set is built around 1236 and 562's floor shape is disabled. Bounded — 1236's own floor lies over the same space and fall recovery exists — but the NOTES' claim "an overlapping layout never captures the body" only holds while the sticky region is alive. No test covers save-while-in-throat or an opcode-18 pose into the overlap (BK00d's L17 pose targets region 1259, outside it).

3. **The combined state with r19-bk is untested.** Fable's 776/777 sweep ran on fd6db6b+Fable only. r19-bk rewrites `l19_sewer_forms.gd` (+245) and its earned test, adds L17 beast-carry poses and L19 floor decks, all of which will now run under region-aware collision and the blocker-skip.
