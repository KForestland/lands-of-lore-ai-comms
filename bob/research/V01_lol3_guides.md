# V01 LoL3 walkthrough and guide scrub

Read-only. No game data was downloaded. No disc image, patch binary, or video was fetched. Lands of Lore III is the 1999 Westwood game (Copper LeGré). Guardians of Destiny is a different game.

A page is A–Z when its own headings or its own walkthrough reach the last portal and the mirror. A page that stops earlier stays partial. Guild quests are extra steps inside one route. They are not four separate campaigns. `landsoflore.de` says Copper may train at all four guilds. Bolle did that so he could write every guild quest. Skipping a guild skips that guild's quests. The portal order stays the shared route.

File stems in O06 (CKEEP, KVOLC, EWOD1, BUPP1, QWOD0, SWOD4) are not joined to these English area names.

## A–Z

- Stijn Bolle, Guide 2.04, 23 August 2000. https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/197755-lands-of-lore-iii/faqs/2527. The same text is mirrored at https://rpggamers.com/walkthrough/lands-of-lore-iii-2. Contents: controls, guilds, walkthrough, items, spells, mixtures, monsters, bugs, cheats. He joined the fighter, magician, cleric, and thief guilds. Portal order in his words: volcano world and the frozen waste, then the Ruloi homeworld and the Underworld, then the shattered desert. The walkthrough reaches Jakel and the seal. Grade for the guide: fan walkthrough. This is the step source for the cards.
- Steve Honeywell, *Lands of Lore III Prima's Official Strategy Guide*, 188 pages. Table of contents: https://lands-of-lore.fandom.com/wiki/Lands_of_Lore_III_Prima%27s_Official_Strategy_Guide. Archive.org full text: https://archive.org/details/Lands_of_Lore_III_Guide. Chapters run from Gladstone and the four guilds (Iron Ring, Talamari, Order of the Finch, Bacchanal, and choosing a familiar) through Copper's rescue, the Draracle, the volcanic caves, the Frozen Wastes, the Underworld and Ruloi, the shattered desert, the NOD base, Cabal, the escape, the confrontation with Jakel, and a separate guild-quest chapter. Grade BRADY for the book. This pass used the contents page. The OCR was not reread page by page. The prior scrub in `lol3_sources.md` already recorded readable OCR pieces: the opening backpack, journal, and rusty knife, and the shattered-desert ending with the Seal and the Shining Path.
- Crash, 1999 area FAQ. The guild index https://the-spoiler.com/RPG/Westwood/landsoflore3.3/Lol3.htm redirects to https://rpggamers.com/walkthrough/lands-of-lore-iii-3. Area pages read on the RPG Gamers mirror: Gladstone `-8`, first shard `-6`, Frozen Wastes `-7`, quests `-11`, Ruloi `-10`, more quests `-9`, Underworld `-12`. Shattered Desert and the final chapter were read at https://the-spoiler.com/RPG/Westwood/landsoflore3.3/desert.htm and https://the-spoiler.com/RPG/Westwood/landsoflore3.3/final.htm. Headings cover every portal area and the seal. Some quest pages are marked optional. Guild initiations are questions inside the guild page, not a second route.
- David Tomsic, quick guide. https://www.gameboomers.com/wtcheats/pcLl/LL3g.htm and the longer copy https://the-spoiler.com/RPG/Westwood/landsoflore32.html. Guild tests, then five portal worlds, Morphera, the Ruloi hand and the hive mother, Jakel, the seal, and the mirror. It is a short A–Z. It is not a room-by-room guide. His count is five shards in five portal worlds.
- Angelfire detailed page. https://www.angelfire.com/darkside/landsoflore/3detail.html. Headings run from Gladstone Woods through the Draracle, the volcanic caves, the Frozen Wastes, the White Tower, the Ruloi world, the final battle, and the seal on the mirror. Maps: https://www.angelfire.com/darkside/landsoflore/3mapdel2.html. Fan page. The indexed text reaches the seal.

## Not a step guide

- https://landsoflore.de/lol3/ — current archive page, German. Story, four guilds, and a historical map. It names six broken portals and six shards of the Spiegel des leuchtenden Pfades. The map labels are Gladstone, Underworld, Shattered Desert, Frozen Wastes, Volcanic Caves, and Ruloi Home World. It says each guild supplies its own familiar, and that training at all four takes longer. It has controls and a hardware note (4 CDs, 10 locations). It has no ordered steps. The March 2023 mirror at https://archive.org/details/landsoflore.de describes older walkthroughs and patch files on the old site. Those files were not downloaded.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lands_of_Lore_III — release dates and the four guilds. No walkthrough.
- https://www.mobygames.com/game/849/lands-of-lore-iii/ — catalog, entry 849. The ad blurbs say five portals in one sentence and six worlds in another. Those two counts stay. No steps.
- https://www.gog.com/game/lands_of_lore_3 — store page. No strategy text. GOG release 23 August 2012.
- https://archive.org/details/landsoflore3 — four disc images plus manual scans. Not opened. Not downloaded.
- Jonathan Riccardi, https://archive.org/details/lands-of-lore-3-part-1 and https://archive.org/details/lands-of-lore-3-secret-treasure-367. Short bug and secret clips. The descriptions are notes, not an A–Z walkthrough.
- https://lands-of-lore.fandom.com/wiki/Lands_of_Lore_III and https://lands-of-lore.fandom.com/wiki/Guilds — series wiki. Guild rank names, not a route.

## Guild names, as each page writes them

Prima contents: Iron Ring, Talamari, Order of the Finch, Bacchanal. Bolle also writes Fighters, Magicians, Cleric, and Thief, and the patch notes write Warrior and Wizard. The German archive writes Magier, Krieger, Priester, and Diebesgilde. Those words stay as written. They are not collapsed into one set of four English class names beyond the four Prima guild titles.

The shared A–Z route does not fork by guild. What changes is the initiation and the later guild quest. Cards for those steps say which guild they belong to.
