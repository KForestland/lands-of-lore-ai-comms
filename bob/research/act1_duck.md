## Required Act 1 path

Chapters 5 through 7, in the book's order. hintbook.txt lines cited were read this pass.

1. Leave the prison, cross the caves, and leave the museum with the summon spell. Chapter 5. The r12x note calls this part of the qualified campaign.
2. Cross the thorn doors. The Hive entrance is past the oil pool. hintbook.txt 2286–2294.
3. In the Hive, drop the hanging rocks to cross the ravine, or cross without them. Fight the spider. The mother is in the alcove by the bones. hintbook.txt 2594–2630. The executioner's breastplate and the ore sword are not required to leave.
4. The village gates open. Visit the shaman. The tavern is last, and that is where you meet Baccata. The thief password is not required. hintbook.txt 2332–2349.
5. Do not cross the long monastery bridge as the Beast. Brother Julian wants two wax copies of the Hive runes and gives the iron flute. Two bits of wax are enough. hintbook.txt 2358–2387.
6. Summon the Hive elevator with the flute. The no-frills path selects floor seven, passes the oil either by stepping through the fire trap or by putting the flute in the slot, passes the blades, lights the brazier, and makes exactly two wax copies. Ride back up and leave. hintbook.txt 2659–2702 and 2817–2822.
7. Give Dawn one copy as you leave the caves. Give Julian the other in the library. Take the Power Orb. Break into the Dark Halls from the storage room, and return. hintbook.txt 2392–2404. The r12x note includes that detour.
8. Trade the Power Orb at the armorer. Accept the gift for Daniel. Find Baccata, follow him to the bridge, and enter the cave into the Savage Jungle. hintbook.txt 2405–2422.

The hidden shrine is optional once the museum summon spell is in hand, hintbook.txt 2296–2302. The purposeful-tourist floors (cat pedestals, the ring of regeneration, the two-boulder healing scroll) are extra. The book says the two rune copies are the only must-take, hintbook.txt 2703–2707 and 2830–2832.
