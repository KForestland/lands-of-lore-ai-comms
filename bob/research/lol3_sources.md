# LoL3 source scrub

Read-only list. Nothing was downloaded. Lands of Lore III is the 1999 Westwood game (Copper LeGré), not Guardians of Destiny. A page is listed only when this pass opened a result that names that game.

## Reference and catalogs

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lands_of_Lore_III — release dates (NA 2 April 1999, EU 26 June 1999), Westwood / Electronic Arts, four guilds, first-person combat. No walkthrough.
- https://www.mobygames.com/game/849/lands-of-lore-iii/ — MobyGames entry 849. Description, credits, screenshots, and release list, including the 23 August 2012 GOG release.
- https://www.mobygames.com/game/849/lands-of-lore-iii/patches/ — patch catalog. v1.06J is dated 7 May 1999. v1.07a is dated 9 May 1999. The notes cover Direct3D crashes and card selection, paletted and transparent textures, cache rebuild, CD-swap and cinematic crashes, Pyra pods, Warrior and Wizard quests, the Frozen Wastes experience bug, a second white-tower shard, Mauri's height, a Lower Gladstone secret room, the prism sword sound, and wrong teleports on the sigil and seal. The new feature is a right-click journal of the last 25 conversations. The files themselves were not fetched.
- https://www.mobygames.com/game/849/lands-of-lore-iii/trivia/ — leftover bugs after the patch, plus the Draracle paintings taken from Guardians of Destiny and the Nod / CABAL final base.
- https://lands-of-lore.fandom.com/wiki/Lands_of_Lore_III_Prima%27s_Official_Strategy_Guide — contents of Steve Honeywell's Prima guide, 188 pages. Chapters run from Gladstone and the four guilds through creatures, items, spells, and Copper's rescue. This page is the table of contents, not the book.
- https://archive.org/details/Lands_of_Lore_III_Guide — Internet Archive full text of that Prima guide. The OCR is rough. Readable pieces include weapon entries (Jade Dagger, Long Sword Prism, Silver Fang, Thohan's Great Sword), survival tips, the opening backpack / journal / rusty knife, and the shattered-desert ending with the Seal and the Shining Path.
- https://archive.org/details/PC_Zone_Issue_079_1999-08_Dennis_Publishing_GB — PC Zone issue 079 (August 1999). Keith Pullin's LoL3 walkthrough: fighter, mage, cleric, and thief guild tests, then the Draracle through the Gladstone Forest volcano portal into Volcania.
- https://www.gog.com/game/lands_of_lore_3 — current store page for the 1999 game. No strategy text.

## Walkthroughs

- https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/197755-lands-of-lore-iii/faqs/2527 — Stijn Bolle, Guide version 2.02 (27 May 2000; GameFAQs copy dated 23 August 2000). Controls, guilds, a walkthrough, items, spells, mixtures, monsters, bugs and fixes, and cheats. It says the Westwood patch fixed crashes, made Pyra pod effects visible, and started the Wizard and Wizard quests, and that bugs remain, including the volcano elevator.
- https://rpggamers.com/walkthrough/lands-of-lore-iii — Lu Richardson. A partial ordered route from the knapsack and rusty knife through Upper Gladstone, the guilds, Dawn, and the Ruloi home world. The author says large stretches are missing because NPCs do not answer.
- https://rpggamers.com/walkthrough/lands-of-lore-iii-2 — the same Bolle 2.02 text, hosted again, including the elevator-bug note and a money trick.
- https://www.gameboomers.com/wtcheats/pcLl/LL3g.htm — David Tomsic, short route: guild tests, the Draracle, Volcania and Morphera, the Ruloi hand and the hive mother.

## Archive.org footage and discs

- https://archive.org/details/landsoflore3 — four disc images (lol3-d1 through lol3-d4) plus manual scans. The upload note says a patch is included. Not opened.
- https://archive.org/details/lands-of-lore-3-part-1 — Jonathan Riccardi, "Secret Walkthrough Guide." Short clips of bugs, a secret passage, and the Sword of Prysm. The description compares the CD version with GOG and names missing spells and shop items. Not a written walkthrough.
- https://archive.org/details/lands-of-lore-3-secret-treasure-367 — another Riccardi set: scarf, Sioned, museum, patch, and crash clips.
