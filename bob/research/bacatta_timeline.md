# Bacatta / Baccata timeline

Grade BRADY for Brady Games lines and HINTBOOK for Prima lines. The books print Baccata. The task title and the tavern F10 print Bacatta. Those spellings stay as printed. No actor, region, or clip was joined. JUNGLE-BACATTA stays the lead's row.

"Friendly and leading after the Jungle" fits only the wake-click at the River Bane bridge, and only until he leaves with the guards. Every later meeting is a choice, a listen, a possible fight, or a death.

## 1. Huline village cantina

Before the bridge. BRADY printed 77: after Shalla is rescued he is in the cantina, a four-armed minion of the law, here to take Luther back to Gladstone. Listen. Luther gets a few hours' grace. Talk to him before Dawn in the Monastery. Walkthrough step 34, printed 169: meet him, talk, agree to meet later near the bridge. Checklist printed 153: find him in the village cantina or in the jungle, then Dawn. HINTBOOK hintbook.txt 2344–2349: the game has not mentioned him; he accompanies Dawn; keep the conversation moving and do not start an attack by accident. A tavern line that turns him hostile contradicts both books.

## 2. River Bane bridge

BRADY printed 86: asleep under a tree once Daniel's knife is in hand. Click to wake him and he escorts across; Hive Warriors destroy the bridge; he leaves with the guards. Attack him and the Gladstone Guards on the far bank must be killed. Printed 88–89: the same split, then he and the guards abandon the east bank. Walkthrough step 52, printed 172: click to wake, follow, watch the destruction, he and the guards leave to find Dawn. This is the only leading stretch, and it ends on the east bank.

## 3. Savage Jungle trap, after the ruins flood

HINTBOOK 3037–3045 and 3864–3865, and the route report's Prima p.90 and p.117: caught in a trap south of the straightest cemetery path; cut him down; he talks and wanders off. HINTBOOK 3476 lists "free Baccata" after the flood, before the Emperor. BRADY printed 103: after the statue, the worm, and the flood, an old friend calls; he hangs upside down from a Wild One trap at eye level. Cut him down, attack him, or leave him. Not automatically friendly, and he does not lead.

## 4. Claw Mountains ice, before the citadel

HINTBOOK 3993, route report Prima p.122: in the twisting ice tunnel, listen, then continue. HINTBOOK 4209: after the citadel exit he has gone. BRADY printed 106: speak with him; the reaction depends on earlier treatment; then the ice bridge toward the citadel. Printed 192: encounter and talk to him before the Ru-Loi Citadel. Walkthrough step 56, printed 211: meet him in the tunnel before the citadel. Not a combat order and not a continued escort.

## 5. City of the Ancients, after the third Belial

BRADY printed 132: after that Hive Lord dies and the far door opens, past actions may produce a meeting, and he may try to stop the Mantle. "After defeating Baccata" the text sends you to the Shrine. HINTBOOK has no Baccata sentence on this beat. Do not merge this with the laboratory.

## 6. Belial's statue, then the laboratory

Two Brady pages disagree. Printed 135: if you have been nice, an OCR-broken "exgmingee" and tragic movie; if you have attacked him, you face the statue alone. Walkthrough step 23, printed 235: if you have abused him earlier, you see him fight the statue for you. HINTBOOK 5410–5413 is a third event, in the laboratory: he has finished another faux-Belial, gives Belial's Horn, says to save Dawn, and dies. The movie, the statue fight, the Mantle block, and the horn are separate.

## Prologue only

BRADY printed 242 says Dawn set out accompanied by the four-armed agent. That is backstory, not a player meeting. The index entry "Baccata, 106" points at the ice-tunnel page.
