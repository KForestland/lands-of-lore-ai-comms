# STORY-049 through STORY-053

Source lines are the route report. Grade HINTBOOK. Place is the Huline Temple. Lane is astra. No Godot. The dreamstone and the two bowls were not given item or prop indices.

## STORY-049

The temple door is just south of that river crossing. Use the dreamstone to enter and take it back. Item: dreamstone. Page: Prima p.129, 131. Acceptance: the production-scene sentence on the row. Depends on STORY-048, which is outside this slice. Unknown: the door control.

## STORY-050

Pick up the dead Huline priest near the entrance, fit him on the stone rectangle, and bring poison and aloe from the pew room to the east then north. Items: the body, poison, aloe. Page: Prima p.132. Acceptance: the same production sentence. Depends on STORY-049. Unknown: the body actor and the two item rows.

## STORY-051

Put aloe in the west bowl and poison in the east bowl, then spark the body so it disappears. Items: aloe west, poison east. Page: Prima p.133. Acceptance: the same production sentence. Depends on STORY-050. Unknown: the two bowl props. The ruins remains bowl is a different bowl.

## STORY-052

North of that poison room, take the green shard from the wooden post. Item: green shard. Page: Prima p.133. Acceptance: the same production sentence. Depends on STORY-051. Unknown: the post and the shard index.

## STORY-053

The book then sends you out to the jungle tree with the raised platform. Spark the torches so stairs rise, open the upper room with the shard, and bring the shard back to the temple. Place: jungle tree, then back. Item: the same shard. Page: Prima p.92, 133. Acceptance: the same production sentence. Depends on STORY-052. Unknown: the tree, the torches, and the upper door.
