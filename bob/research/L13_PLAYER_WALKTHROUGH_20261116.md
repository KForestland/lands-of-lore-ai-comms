## The elevator and the web, first

The elevator is not a cutscene to the boss.

The book (4097-4116) says: spark the globe in the elevator so it descends, and on the bottom level the real Ruloi fight begins. The boss is later, after the Ruloi are dead, the maidens are out, the pink stairs are climbed, and the rippling bridge is crossed (4145-4171). The boss door then plays "special scene elements" and dialogue before the fight.

The longplay agrees. From `g_00187` (3:22:40, Remaining Ruloi 19/19, HUD up) through `g_00200` (3:29:10, 5/19, a Ruloi in the air, HUD up) the player is fighting in ordinary play. No elevator movie is on that grid. The HUD-off close-up is `g_00214` (3:36:10), a creature filling the frame. The fight, HUD back, is `g_00216` (3:37:10), in front of a bright green field. That is about fourteen minutes after he is already inside and counting Ruloi.

Our elevator is the same kind of object as the book: a deck he rides. `l13_elevator.gd` moves floors 147, 148, 149, 153, 154, 159, and 163 between heights -40 and -1040. Spark 1 or Spark 3, or E, starts it. Standing on the deck, he is carried. The screen says "The elevator descends." or "The elevator rises." The prompt is "E - Use elevator brain" or, from the upper floor when it is down, "E - Call elevator". The journal hint is "The elevator brain responds to Spark (1, then Q)." It does not play a movie and it does not move him to the boss.

Our web is a different object, and it is the stop he hit. `l13_belial.gd` watches a web at (-5482, 4, -155). E is offered only when actors 21 through 39 are all at health 0 (`l13_prison.gd` `all_ruloi_dead`, nineteen actors, the same 19 the counter shows). Until then the aimed line is "Something still lives here." If actor 22 still has health, the line adds "One waits above the lift." (`l13_action_hint_targets.gd`). That sentence is ours. The book never says it. The book says some Ruloi retire to the upper level, and he climbs the ramp and takes the teleporter back upstairs to finish them (4060-4062). They are not supposed to come down the lift. After the nineteen are dead, our E is "E - Enter the web doorway", movie 31 plays, and he is moved to (-2974, -1108, -2680). The screen then says "The creature waits beyond the web. Cast Healing III to counter its poison." So the movie to the boss is the web, and only after the count is zero. The lift is the earlier ride.

r19o4 matches that gate. He rode the lift. The web refused him because actor 22 was still alive above the lift. The book would send him back upstairs for that Ruloi. It would not have him wait under the lift for 22 to descend, and it would not call the lift the way to the boss.
