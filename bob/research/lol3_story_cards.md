# LoL3 story cards

Read-only. No game was run. No opcode, item type, or block id is assigned. O06 file stems are not area names.

The step source is Stijn Bolle, Guide 2.04, https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/197755-lands-of-lore-iii/faqs/2527. Grade for that guide: fan walkthrough. Prima chapter titles are BRADY, from https://lands-of-lore.fandom.com/wiki/Lands_of_Lore_III_Prima%27s_Official_Strategy_Guide. Crash's area pages and Tomsic's quick guide are second sentences. Where they disagree, both sentences stay.

Numbering is LOL3, in the STORY card shape. These numbers are not LoL2 STORY ids.

The portal route is shared. Guild steps are marked with the guild. Bolle's portal order is volcano and frozen waste, then Ruloi and the Underworld, then the shattered desert. Tomsic prefers the Frozen Wastes shard before Volcania. Crash says the third quest of each guild is offered after the Ruloi homeworld is closed, and that doing the Underworld first removes those quests because Gladstone is gone except the fighter training ground. landsoflore.de says six portals and six shards. Tomsic says five shards in five portal worlds. Those counts stay unmerged.

## STORY-L3-001

Area: woods, after the opening. Prima chapter: Copper's Rescue.
NPC: Dawn, after two guards bring Copper to Gladstone.
Item: knapsack, journal, rusty knife. Richardson and Tomsic. A spider web is cut with the knife.
Guild paths: none yet. The same opening for every guild.
Source: Bolle section "1 The Beginning". https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/197755-lands-of-lore-iii/faqs/2527. Richardson: https://rpggamers.com/walkthrough/lands-of-lore-iii.
Bolle: the Draracle leaves, rift dogs take the soul, Copper wakes in an unknown part of the woods. Dawn tells him to join a guild and find the Draracle's caves. Grade for the Dawn line: the Bolle page.

## STORY-L3-002

Area: Upper Gladstone, then Gladstone Woods, then back.
NPC: guild greeters in the next cards. A boar in the woods.
Item: pork chop from the boar.
Guild paths: the chop is the Finch initiation item. Other guilds do not use it.
Source: Bolle "2 Upper Gladstone" and "3 Gladstone Woods".
Bolle: chase the boar, hit it with the knife, take the pork chop, return to Upper Gladstone. Richardson: boars at the docks also leave pork chops. Both sentences stay.

## STORY-L3-008

Area: volcano world, then the Draracle's keep. Prima: Finding the Draracle.
NPC: the Draracle, then Jakel through the right-hand door.
Item: Golden Sigil. The mirror in the back of Jakel's room is the one to restore.
Guild paths: shared.

Richardson: step off the arrival portal or be pulled back to the woods, use a lamplight egg, look down, and meet the Draracle on the path. Both sentences stay.

## STORY-L3-009

Area: volcanic caves, inner chamber, then the lava chamber. Prima: The Volcanic Caves.
NPC: Morphea on the Crash page. Morphera on Tomsic's page and in the MobyGames voice credit (Diane Farr). The two spellings stay.
Item: the shard at the lava pool. Bolle uses the sigil on a shard. Crash says pick the shard up from the base of the pond.

## STORY-L3-011

Area: Frozen Wastes. Portal on the north side of the forest by the big lake. Crash. Bolle: near the waterfall. White Tower at the north of that land.
NPC: Jacinda. Rix and Morrison are on the road. Crash.
Item: the shard. White Tower Key and Silver Fang Hilt are on Jacinda's body in Bolle's kill path. Four gems restore a force field. Candle order Bolle gives, left to right: blue, red, green, pink. Button order: blue, green, red, pink. Chesara's medallion is a fighter quest item used in this tower, not the shard.
Guild paths: the shard is shared. The medallion door is the Iron Ring quest. See STORY-L3-017.
Source: Bolle frozen-waste section. Crash, https://rpggamers.com/walkthrough/lands-of-lore-iii-7.
Bolle: kill Jacinda, take the key and the hilt, then use the sigil on the shard. Trusting her leaves Copper a prisoner, and a menu button then plays the movies. Crash: agree to help her, and the shard is still taken from her body because she attacks at the end. Take the key before the shard. Both outcomes stay.

## STORY-L3-013

Area: Ruloi home world. Crash: the grey circle between the Upper and Lower Gladstone entrances. Richardson: the circular structure in the middle of the woods map.
NPC: Griselda meets Copper at the entrance on the Crash page. The hive mother is the end of Tomsic's hand path. Richardson writes an Amazon in a jail to the north. Crash and Bolle write Sioned in a prison, and that meeting is the Talamari quest, STORY-L3-019. The Amazon and Sioned stay unmerged.
Item: Ruloi hand. Regeneration devices. The shard, taken with the sigil. Bolle.
Guild paths: the shard is shared. Sioned's prison is Talamari only.
Source: Crash, https://rpggamers.com/walkthrough/lands-of-lore-iii-10. Tomsic. Bolle's Ruloi section. Richardson.
Tomsic: keep killing yellow Ruloi warriors until the hand appears, destroy the pads, and the hand opens a hidden telepad to the hive mother. Crash: the hand is also in a sewer secret room with the Great Sword Darkstorm, and it opens two locked rooms. Both sentences stay. Bolle: destroy every regeneration device or the Ruloi keep returning. A crystal-device sample of the shard is the wizard quest, and it is done before the sigil click.

## STORY-L3-014

Area: the Underworld. Portal near the Lower Gladstone entrance, by the Geldenberry farm. Crash.
NPC: Dash, only when the Bacchanal was joined. He gives the clock-key quest. The creature in the dark room. Bolle calls the freed figure the soul of Copper's father. Crash calls the kill the Scythe Demon. Those two names stay.
Item: clock key, used three times on the grandfather clock between the two elevators. The shard, with the sigil. Bolle.
Guild paths: the shard is shared. Dash and the clock key are Bacchanal only.
Source: Bolle underworld section. Crash, https://rpggamers.com/walkthrough/lands-of-lore-iii-12.

Crash: a scythe demon, and the clue is that a ghost does not like sunlight. Crash also says to finish shopping in Upper and Lower Gladstone first, because this world destroys them. His special note on the frozen-waste page says the third guild quests vanish if the Underworld is done before Ruloi. Bolle's portal sentence still allows either of those two worlds before the desert. Both sentences stay.

## Where a guide stops

PC Zone's unknown line starts once the volcano portal is entered. Richardson's unknown line is the stretches she says the NPCs never answer. The Computer Show page and Kerstin's page have no ending in the extracts read here, so their unknown line is everything after the woods tasks those extracts contain. Prima's chapter titles cover the shared route. Room-by-room Prima text was not entered. No card invents a portal-to-stem join.
