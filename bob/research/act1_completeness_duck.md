# Act 1 completeness duck

Player order from the Prima hintbook, chapters 1–7 (printed folio = form-feed count minus 12). Chapters 1–4 are the introduction, the spell and item lists, and the controls. They are not a route. Every Act 1 sentence in them is attached to the later beat that uses it. The ordered play is chapter 5 (Cave, Museum), chapter 6 (Huline Jungle, village, monastery, Dark Halls, shore departure), and chapter 7 (Hive), with the Hive chapter visited twice from chapter 6.

Read-only. Grades use only `playtest_feedback_20261023/TRIAGE_20261103.md`, `grok_research/source_ids_act1_pt3.md`, and `~/opus_work/lol2_r13/docs/`.

- HOSTED: an r13 doc says that player-facing step is in the port, and the 2026-11-03 triage does not report that same step failing.
- MISSING: the triage lists it as absent or broken, or an r13 doc says it is not hosted or not gameplay-integrated. An older “implemented” sentence stays in the evidence when the owner’s play disagrees.
- UNKNOWN: none of the three sources decides the step. An unread opcode in the source-id pack is cited beside the owner row. It does not by itself grade the port.

Stories stay with Astra. This file does not build anything.
