## Problems

1. **Stale comment / changed behaviour in the oil-chain test** — `tests/l16_se_oil_chain_earned_test.gd:4` says "Region 4947 rises 70 over a 48 run (55 degrees). The body slides onto region 4946." After this patch the quad leaves the collision mesh and the body walks down a 40° ramp instead of sliding. The descent should still pass (gentler, still lands on 4946, no fall reset), but the comment is now wrong and — more importantly — the lip is now climbable *upward* from 4946, which it was not before. Recommend re-running `l16_se_oil_chain_earned_test` after merge and updating the comment.
2. **Minor fragility in `slab()`** (`l16_terrace_lips.gd`, patch line 57-74): it splits quad vertices by mean-y and bails unless exactly 2 low + 2 high. If any owned face ever changed shape, `owns()` would still strip its collision while no prism is built, leaving a hole. All ten current faces are 4-point 30/100 quads, so this is latent only.
