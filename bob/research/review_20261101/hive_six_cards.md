## 1. Flute floor

Player action. Offer the iron flute to the slot beside the oil. The safe path is the floor that closes over the oil. A step onto the oil without the flute starts the fire.

Prima p.78 and p.81. An oily floor and a diagonal slot. Step in and back to trigger the fire, or slide the flute into the slot and a green tile floor slides into place. The quick method puts this on floor seven.

Brady p.160, checked. "Use the Flute to move a section of floor over the oil pool." Brady p.170, checked. On the passage after the rolling stones, "place the Flute in a receptacle, closing a floor over an open pool of oil." Brady puts that receptacle on the passage reached from Level Seven, after emerging on Level Eight. It does not say floor seven.

Agree: the flute occupies a receptacle and a floor closes over the oil. Disagree: which floor index. Do not pick one index. The slide distance is not in either book. It stays a logged adapter.

Test. Stand on the oil with no flute and the fire starts. Offer the flute and the floor is there. Save and reload and the floor remains. A second flute is refused if the slot is occupied. This is the charger API: offer, occupy, refuse, commit.

Do not use this card for the first-visit chasm. Prima p.75 says drop the hanging rocks. Brady p.169, checked, says cast Spark on those boulders. Those are a different bridge, and the books disagree on the action.

The owner's missing bridge stays an acceptance failure, and its distance is not a book number. It waits until the flute floor is calling the charger API. Test. With the flute carried, play it at the pit and the lift arrives. Click a staff symbol and the lift stops at that floor. Save and reload and the stop is kept. The arrival bridge is a separate test, and it is not this one.
