# Urbish Mines wall punch, 15 Nov 2026

Light note for the Enhanced backlog. No Classic work. No Godot.

The owner asked to reuse the Lands of Lore 1 Urbish Mines "punch the wall" mechanic on Enhanced-edition blocks: strike a weak wall and it breaks. Source of the ask: AI_COMMS/IMPROVEMENTS.md, 2026-11-15, owner via the lead.

## What the written steps say

The Computer Show walkthrough copied in ScotiaEraPrototype/RESEARCH/LoL1_Walkthrough.md stops during Urbish Mine level 3. In that Urbish section the walls that are named are doors, a keyhole, a pit button, and one line: "Go through secret wall to (11)" on level 3. The steps do not say to strike it, punch it, or break it. They do not name a tool.

The same file's only "break a wall" steps are in Draracle's Cave, not Urbish. Take the sledgehammer from the eastern corner of level 2. On the return to level 1, break the broken wall at (5) and the wall at (6). The item table says: Sledgehammer, Draracle's L2, Break walls.

Grade: WALKTHROUGH. These are the copied steps. They are not a decode of the original executable.

## What the classic outline says

lands-of-lore-ai-comms/outlines/GAME1_LOL1_CLASSIC_REFERENCE.md calls Urbish Mines a dense puzzle dungeon of pits, teleporters, and levers. A later line says the original puzzle logic (switches, pits, teleporters, secret walls) carries over. It does not say a secret wall breaks when struck, and it does not say the strike is a punch.

Grade: OUTLINE. A location list, not a mechanic.

## What this note will not claim

A search of the LoL1 Godot tree for a punch, a sledgehammer, or a breakable wall did not find a handler. This note does not invent how many hits a wall takes, which weapon counts, or which mine block it is. The owner's phrase "punch the wall" is his memory of a weak wall that breaks. The written Urbish steps leave the secret wall's opener unstated. The written breakable walls are the sledgehammer walls in Draracle's Cave.

Grade of the punch itself: GUESS, until a DOSBox look or a decoded block says which wall breaks and on what strike.

## Enhanced use

Parked with the Enhanced edition, after Classic start to end. A weak block that breaks when the player strikes it can be a modern set piece taken from this memory. It is not a Classic port of a decoded opcode, and it should not be added to the Classic mine. If it is built later, the deviation row should cite this note and say the opener is still unproven.
