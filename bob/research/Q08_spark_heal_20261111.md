## What a new Luther pays

PROVEN for the debit. `0xD1060` loads byte0 into esi. Three later adjustments do not touch Spark effects 20–24:

- `0xD1068`: if player `+0x19A` bit `0x20` is set and the effect is 0, 1, 2, 3 or 4, esi loses a quarter. Spark is outside that set.
- `0xD1098`: if `+0x19B` has bits `0x20`, `0x40` and `0x80` and the effect is 30–34, esi is halved. Spark is outside that set.
- `0xD10D5`: if player `+0x1AB` is 2, esi is halved (`sar` by 1).

`0xCFA77` stores 0 at `+0x1AB` in the same init that stores pool cap 20 at `0xCFB18`. The half at `0xD10D5` does not run. The debit is the full byte0: 25, 50, 75, 150, 250.

`0xD10ED` reads flag `+0x72`. It was set to 1 at `0xD0F61`. Nonzero means compare esi with dword `[player+0x145]`. If esi is at most that dword, `0xD256C` subtracts it. If esi is greater, `0xD1105` stores 0. A new Luther's current pool is 20 (Q03, `0xCFCFE`). Spark 1's 25 is above 20, so the store is 0. Spark 2–5 are above 20 as well.

The cast still reaches that debit after the effect. Spark cases `0xD1AB9`, `0xD1B1C`, `0xD1B7F`, `0xD1BE1` and `0xD1C44` jump to `0xD1134`. That tail falls through to `0xD1033`, which loads byte0 again and then enters `0xD10ED`. The jump is not a skipped payment.

## Spark 5 on the caster

PROVEN from `0xD1C44`. The case clears the pay flag, then:

- If dword `[player+0x195]` is nonzero, it jumps to `0xD1D25` with the pay flag still 0. The debit is skipped.
- If byte `[0x23C3F]` is nonzero, `0x7C5DC(0x23819, 0)` runs and the pay flag stays 0. Otherwise `0xD1C9B` sets the pay flag, and the tail debits 250.
- If dword `[player+0x17D]` is 0, it spawns class `0x5E` through `0x104DA0` and stores 1 at health `+0x14D` (`0xD1CDB`).

## Wizard level

The book's "costs fall as wizard level rises" was not found on the mana dword. `0xD2494` stores byte1, the reward base, at frame `+0x1E`, and copies it to esi. `0xD11A6` subtracts byte `[player+0x162]` (the second index, 1 on a new Luther) and jumps through `0x77EE8` when the sum stays in 0..7. Spark's bases are 12 or more, so the index is above 7 and `0xD2532` runs. That scales esi, and `0xD255D` may pass it to `0xD8B94`. `0xD1033` then loads byte0 again for the debit. The scale does not survive into the mana subtract.
