## 2. RE status

Decoded, from O06. WSX peel 51 of 52 DAT files. LMF record table at `0x100`, 16-byte records, sampled count 38, marker `0xCDCDCDCD`. `.map` is that table. CKEEP field 0 is repeating 8-byte `(u16 0, i16 x, u16 0, i16 z)`, about 341 points. Field 2 floor polygons are partial. CONVERT `0x028E` / `0x828E` span rows exact-consume on 26 of 26 mip0 payloads. Grey index pictures show silhouettes. KVOLC slot 3 is 126 by 91. Flag `0x008B` is raw 8bpp when `size == w * h`. 25 PCM wavs. 9 of 19 MOVIES1–4 entries are VQA to webm. Companion suffixes `.map`, `.odf`, `.lmf`, `.tex` are string names.

Missing, from O06. Colour. The bank palette at `+0x6A2`, scaled by 4, is rainbow noise. The RGB555 bake is an unread `u16[256]` LUT. The CKEEP CONVERT bank for `xtb5door2` is missing. A VGA palette PNG is not that LUT. The 16-byte leaf is not known to be vertices. `.tex` / LEVED is a separate format. Door `xtb5door2` is open. Retail packs are not whole-file LZO. 10 MOVIES3/4 entries are unidentified. One WSX DAT is unpeeled. `lol3_extracted` has no rebuild script. No LoL3 opcode was entered. QWOD0 and SWOD4 notes stay container structure.

LOL3_CLASSIC_PREP adds the same colour gap and names the next capture as GL upload or winedbg at `0x4826D0`. That capture is not this chunk. It needs the retail tree, which this plan does not mount.
