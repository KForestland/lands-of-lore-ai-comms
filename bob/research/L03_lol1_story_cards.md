# L03 LoL1 story cards

No Godot was run. These are not backlog ids. The step source is `~/ScotiaEraPrototype/RESEARCH/LoL1_Walkthrough.md` (The Computer Show walkthrough, copied for Scotia Era). It stops during Urbish Mine level 3 and says the rest continues. `~/lands-of-lore-ai-comms/outlines/GAME1_LOL1_CLASSIC_REFERENCE.md` is a location list. Where the two disagree, both sentences stay.

The reference puts the Draracle before the poisoning. The walkthrough does Roland, then the poisoning, then the Draracle. The cards follow the walkthrough. The reference order is not used.

The walkthrough calls Kieran balanced and Conrad a rogue. The reference calls Kieran the fast one who cannot wear boots, and Conrad balanced. The specialties stay unknown.

## Cards

LOL1-01. Pick one of Ak'shel, Michael, Kieran, or Conrad. Place: opening. Acceptance: a new game has that hero. Unknown: which specialty is which.

LOL1-02. At Gladstone, hear the king, take the King's Writ from Geron, and take the Magic Atlas from the library. Place: Gladstone Keep. Acceptance: the writ and the atlas are carried, then the north gate opens. The walkthrough also buys a mace. That shop stop is not marked required.

LOL1-03. In the thugs' hideout, take the lantern, the thugs' key, and the lock picks. Place: Northland Forest. Acceptance: those three are carried. The rock, plate, and button order is in the walkthrough and was not copied here.

LOL1-04. At the Grey Eagle, take the compass and Timothy joins. At Roland's manor, read the burnt scroll, pass the ogre by bluff or by fight, and find Roland. The ruby is already gone. Place: Southland Forest. Acceptance: Timothy is in the party and Roland's scene has played. Unknown: which ogre choice the original requires.

LOL1-05. Return to the throne room. Scotia poisons King Richard. Dawn keeps him alive and gives a spell book. Baccata joins and Timothy leaves. Place: Gladstone. Acceptance: Timothy is gone, Baccata is in the party, the king is poisoned. The walkthrough says to notice a fallen cup and a shield shape. What the player must do with them is not written.

LOL1-06. Through Draracle's caves, rescue Lora from a pod, reach the Draracle, and take the riddle scroll. Lora stays. The walkthrough says the elixir is mixed in a tower. Place: Draracle's Caves. Acceptance: the riddle scroll is carried and Lora has left. Unknown: the two path orders beyond the walkthrough's own lever list. Key items named there: sledgehammer, emerald eye, sapphire eye, red key, bezel ring.

LOL1-07. After the caves, the keep has fallen and the party crosses Lake Dread. Victor says Dawn already took the boat. Place: Northland, Lake Dread. Acceptance: the party is across the lake. This "fallen" line is a second return. It is not the same sentence as the poisoning scene.

LOL1-08. In Opinwood, take the green skull. Give the beggar money. Dawn's wagon will not accept the party yet. Place: Opinwood. Acceptance: the green skull is carried and the wagon has refused. The lightning scroll and the long sword are picked up in the same chapter. They are not marked required.

LOL1-09. In the Gorkha swamp, the witch doctor translates the riddle. Take the brass helmet from the stickmen and give it to the chieftain. He gives the Ruby of Truth. Place: Gorkha Swamp. Acceptance: the ruby is carried. Freeze is how the walkthrough crosses the pits. Unknown: whether another crossing exists.

LOL1-10. Show Dawn the ruby and the riddle. She gives empty flasks and the pyramid key. Place: Opinwood, Dawn's wagon. Acceptance: the pyramid key is carried.

LOL1-11. Urbish Mine, as far as the walkthrough is written. Use the green skull on the giant slug. Take the silver key on level 3 and throw fireballs at the nests. Place: Urbish Mine. Acceptance: the nests are burned. The text stops there. Unknown: the rest of the mine, the mask temple, and whether Paulson joins. The party list says he joins. The steps never say he joins.

LOL1-12. White Tower. The walkthrough names three events and does not order them: mix the elixir, cure King Richard, confront Scotia. Place: White Tower, in that stub only. Acceptance: not written. The reference instead puts Jana, Lyle, and a pit to a hidden floor here, and does not put the last fight here.

LOL1-13. Castle Cimmeria. The walkthrough says several levels, Scotia's final form, and the end. Place: Castle Cimmeria. Acceptance: not written. The reference says the fight uses the Whole Truth, made from the Ruby of Truth and a Shard of Truth. The walkthrough never names the shard or the Whole Truth. Those two endings stay unmerged.

LOL1-14. Upper Opinwood, Yvel, and the Catwalk are in the reference and not in the written steps. The reference says Vaelan's Cube opens Scotia's barrier, a hornet nest is an ingredient, Yvel has Sadie and Bruno, and the Catwalk has Frendor and Mylek. No player step is given. Unknown: where each of those sits between LOL1-10 and LOL1-13.
