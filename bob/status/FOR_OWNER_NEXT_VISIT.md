# For your next visit (lead, 13 November 2026, written 13:40)

## Play this: the Dracoid Cemetery on build r19g

```
~/lol2_playtests/play_r19g.sh
```

Press **F2** and choose **"Act 2 - Cemetery, main gate opened (full health)"**. Play it as a normal player, as far as you get. Press **F10** where something is wrong or you are stuck, and type a few words.

New in this build (Cemetery only):
- Chests, crypt tops, doors and breakable fronts now work (E or left click).
- The two chargers store and give orbs; globes go in the tombs.
- The lower-level rails: walk up to the rail and press **W + Space**. They are one-way; the way back is by the stairs.
- The tomb floor that the oil blast opens no longer drops you into a void.
- The fall at "not ok here" is closed by two walls.

Known and not fixed yet:
- The orb holder you missed at (468, -1805) is not in this build; nobody has found it in the data yet.
- Spells beyond Spark and Healing are not in yet (spell core is Sol's next task).
- The robot has not walked the whole Cemetery clean to the ankh; its last full walk found the void under the tomb floor (now fixed) and then failed on its own route.
