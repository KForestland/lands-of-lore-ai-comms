# Cemetery try-list

One walk, from the gate to the Emperor. Press only what this page names, in this order. The census marks many more globes, chests, and gargoyles. They are not this walk.

Start at the Cemetery gate.

1. In the yard, open the two crypt tops.
2. Go down the ramp. Take the empty glass globe beside the three-clawed gold charger. Put it in the charger and take it back yellow.
3. Come back up to the yellow gargoyle in front of the door to the chests. Offer the yellow globe. Open the chest inside and take the empty globe. Open the chest just beyond it.
4. Charge that empty globe at the gold charger.
5. Offer the yellow globe at the yellow gargoyle by the south door of that wing. Walk through the middle of the doorway. Take the empty globe on the floor in front of the chest. Open the chest further in.
6. Charge that empty globe at the gold charger. Keep this yellow globe.
7. Leave the supply tomb's yellow gargoyle shut. That yellow globe is for the tomb you open after the mirror.
8. At the courtyard's yellow gargoyle, offer the yellow globe. Go through the door and take the empty globe on the floor. Come back out and charge it, so you are holding a yellow globe again.

South gate into the jungle, then down the pit into the Ruins.

9. Spark the magic bridge and cross it. Enter the Mage's Tower. Spark the orb that opens the white stair and climb it. Spark the orbs at the top, defeat the guardian in the chamber, spark the shield, and take the Bracers of the Dead.
10. Leave the tower by the foot path. Spark the mirror doors and take the mirror back to the Cemetery.
11. Offer the yellow globe at the gold gargoyle there. Take the two empty globes inside.
12. Use the winged statue's mirror to return to the Ruins, climb to the white charger, and charge both globes white.
13. Come down and follow the tower foot out. Do not cut across the open pit. Spark the mirror doors again and return to the Cemetery.
14. Offer a white globe at the white gargoyle. Inside, use the switch so the barrel tips, and take the empty globe in that room. Follow the barrel and take the empty globe behind it. Spark the oil on the floor.
15. At the blue charger below, charge one empty globe blue.
16. Cross the two fences. Offer the blue globe at the blue gargoyle of the priest's tomb. With the bracers on, speak to the priest and take his urn.

Back through the statue mirror to the Ruins chapel.

17. Break the north rail, cross to the chapel, and place the priest's body on the pyre. Spark the pyre. Take the filled urn.
18. Return to the priest and trade the ashes for the ankh.
19. Set the ankh on the slab beside the Emperor's tomb, then take the ankh back. The tomb opens.
20. Downstairs, set a yellow globe, a white globe, and a blue globe on the three stands. Use the ankh on the inner door. Take the Emperor's remains.

The screen should name the thing under the crosshair before you press E. Spark is the spark spell. A door opens when the gargoyle's colour matches the globe you offer.
