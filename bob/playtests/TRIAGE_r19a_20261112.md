## What this changes (lead)

1. **The owner's clipping is mostly a drawing problem, not a collision problem.** 13 of the 37 reports are faces of other rooms drawn where they should not be (a slab floating in the air in the Temple, a black hole where the next room should show, ice walls in the sky in the Claw Mountains). Only 6 are about what is solid. The review of 12 November said "keep drawing as it is and fix collision first"; for these levels that was the wrong order. The collision rebuild (G-05) stays, but it will not remove what he sees.
2. **13 reports are knowledge of the original** that we cannot get from the data: a teleporter and a vault opener in the Citadel, the spider eruption in the Temple, the gas that should blow up three doors, the box that goes in a slot and the ivory chips in the City, the blood altar that drains. These become rows in the object table with the owner as the source.
3. He reached seven levels in about 70 minutes from the F2 starts. The starts and the F10 bundles work.
