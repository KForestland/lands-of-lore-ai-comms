# MASTER STORY BIBLE — CORE ENTRY 1
## The Asynchronia / Chronowalker Thread — Canon Lock

*This is the single most important continuity thread in the entire series.
It connects Chronicles of Time to Chronicles of Souls and explains
Asynchronia's origin, which was previously left as an unexplained "paradox
child." Lock this before writing anything else that touches either hub.*

---

## THE TWO BEINGS

**The Chronomancer** — an ancient Dracoid, far older than the series' human
timeline. Not disguised as human — actually appears human (or near enough)
to everyone she deals with, her true Dracoid nature hidden. She exists
within Chronicles of Time as Asynchronia's mentor/guide figure. Her own
origin and full history are deliberately left in shadow — an old mystery,
not one this thread needs to resolve.

**Asynchronia** — a girl who does not yet exist when Chronicles of Time
begins, and yet is there from the start. This is the whole of it:

---

## THE TIMELINE, IN ORDER OF WHAT HAPPENS TO HER (not necessarily the order she experiences it)

1. **LoL 6, deep future relative to CoT:** Eina and Aldric have a daughter.
2. **In the original timeline**, that daughter is erased — pulled out of
   existence. (The specifics of *why* are yours to lock later — likely tied
   to the ring/Seal reunion in LoL 6's climax, or to the paradox of a child
   born from two separate worlds meeting across the mirror. Worth deciding
   deliberately, not defaulting.)
3. **The Chronomancer reaches back** — impossibly far back, into the era of
   Chronicles of Time (LoL 1–3) — and saves her. Not "saves" as in rescues
   in place; she pulls the girl out of her own erasure and sets her down in
   an era centuries before her own birth would occur. This is where she
   becomes **Asynchronia** — a name and identity assigned to her in an era
   that isn't hers, by the being who saved her.
4. **She lives Chronicles of Time as Asynchronia** — genuinely present in
   and shaping LoL 1–3's events, mentored by the Chronomancer, exactly as
   currently written. She does not yet know she is anyone's daughter, or
   that she was ever erased.
5. **Across LoL 4, 5, and 6**, she is not present as a character — but she
   is *present as an influence*. The clues, hints, and traces that
   Chronicles of Souls already attributes to "the Chronowalker" — the Echo
   Stone symbol Eina draws for years, the warning to Copper, the watching
   presence throughout LoL 5 and 6 — these are her, reaching forward from
   her life in the CoT era toward the moment of her own birth and erasure,
   slowly working to stabilize a timeline that currently doesn't hold
   together. She is trying to save herself, from centuries away, in the only
   direction available to her.
6. **In LoL 6**, through the events of that game, she finally succeeds. Her
   timeline stabilizes. She stops being a fragile echo holding two identities
   at once and simply *is* — real, singular, whole. This is also, not
   coincidentally, the moment Eina and Aldric's daughter is no longer erased
   in any timeline: past (as Asynchronia) and present (as their daughter)
   collapse into one continuous, real life.
7. **Afterward**, fully real for the first time, she takes on the mentor
   role that the Chronomancer once played for her — recruiting a new
   student for the next unification: **the connecting hero of Chronicles of
   Souls.** Her hub for this work is Draicele — specifically the version of
   the Thomgog/Draicele city established in LoL 4, matching the "hub across
   time" concept the Chronicles of Souls document already outlines. The new
   student's job: repairing the souls of Gladstone and Valdenmere, the two
   wounds LoL 5/6 leave open.

## UPDATE — HER NAME (from LoL6)

The Chronowalker's birth name is confirmed: **Katrine**. She learns it
herself in LoL6's epilogue by attending her own birth and overhearing Eina
and Aldric name the infant she also is. Full detail, including the
identity question this opens (is Eina now more like a sister? is the
Chronomancer her "real" mother in every sense but blood?), is in
`GAME6_LOL6_CLASSIC_REFERENCE.md`.

## UPDATE — LoL4 CONCEPT PASS (2026-09-20)

- **Chronowalker in LoL4:** never on screen. Her traces are echo stones and a
  hidden gallery in Draicele with two paintings of one woman — young (as
  Asynchronia in Chronicles of Time) and at about forty (as the Chronowalker)
  — facing each other. Copper wonders aloud who she is. This stays inside the
  "influence only" rule above.
- **Draicele in LoL4** is itself an experiment about souls, and souls echo
  through true time, consistent with the one-working-two-halves reading of
  the mirror-world counterpart in LoL6.
