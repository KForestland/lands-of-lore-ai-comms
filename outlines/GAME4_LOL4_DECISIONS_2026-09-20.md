## Decisions

### Blocking questions

**D1 [DECIDED] Named antagonist (was 13) — Melicothor.**
Male. In his youth a good citizen of Yvel (city confirmed 2026-09-20, from LoL1). After Scotia he researched artifacts and found the location of Draicele. Obsessive and cold; every word calculated. Irritated by Elyra's chipper humor. He meets Elyra as **Raven the Artificer**.
*Answer to Q7:* he appears at random places after Act 1, at the start of Act 2, like Scotia in LoL1.
*AI call:* three encounters at random unvisited nodes from a five-node pool (`DRC_dune_avenue`, `DRC_platform_yard`, `DRC_forge_district`, `DRC_vault_approach`, `DRC_echo_stones`), and a fixed reveal at `DRC_ward_court` right after Choice B.

**D2 [DECIDED] Mandatory Baccata flashbacks (was 14).**
Confirmed as Baccata's memories (2026-09-20). LoL1: saving Dawn, and seeing Elyra as her daughter. LoL2: his death, and Belial as the true evil.
*AI call on placement:* the Echo Chamber carries the LoL1 pair; Draicele memory site A carries his death; site B carries Belial as the true evil.

**D3 [DECIDED] Neutral climax priority (was 15).**
Neither cost is the default. The game asks the player who should bear the cost: Baccata loses his memories, or Copper remains as Draicele's eternal guardian. Never both.

**D4 [DECIDED] Permanent-death NPC list (was 16).**
A shopkeeper, the leader, and one of the Huline mothers, who die when `seed_loss=high`.
*Answer to Q3 (you make them up):* all at Last Haven. **Marshal Oswin Tull** (leader), **Bran Kettle** (shopkeeper), **Sunna** (Huline mother, son Pip). AI-named, easy to change.

**D5 [DECIDED] Azure Spire scope.**
Optional. Its locked guardians, stone golems from the Golden Age, are gatekeepers on the last floor bound to the lich. They are ancient Draicele servants. Clues show how to free them. Freed golems appear at the ending and help.
*Answer to Q4:* the user's earlier paragraph was lost in the drafting process, so this description is the source now.
*AI calls:* freeing is independent of Purify or Corrupt. The clues are three release words from the three mini dungeons: Human "Leash", Huline "Fall", Dracoid "Servants, stand". Freed golems join the spirit allies. If not freed they fight at the Spire and do not appear at the ending.

### Grey-area questions

**D6 [DECIDED] Luther/Dawn mid-game (was 17).**
A bird from Luther carries a speaking stone and delivers messages that lift Elyra's spirits, plus general doting. Answer to Q5: both speak on the speaking stone.
*AI call:* four messages. At the hut, after the Canyons rest, before the Draicele crossing, and one for Copper in his tower. Replaces the single found letter in SQ-G2.

**D7 [DECIDED] Romance scope (was 18).** No romance options. Lira is a friend and ally only.

**D8 [DECIDED] Copper crown-refusal fame (was 19).**
He left a letter saying he must research souls and make peace with his own, and that he cannot become king because he is broken.

**D9 [DECIDED] Evil Dawn armor setup (was 20).**
Cold open. Dawn arrives through a portal from Gladstone and appears in front of Elyra while Copper is hunting her. Answer to Q6: it is the post-credits sting.

**D10 [DECIDED] Chronowalker crumb (was 21).**
Most present around Draicele: echo stones, and a hidden room with a Draicele painting that shows her young (from CoT) and older (about forty) facing each other. No live presence. Copper wonders aloud who she is, because of the echo stones.

**D11 [DECIDED] Companion climax lines (was 22).**
The Huline scout and the human alchemist get a shout-out in the epilogue. If Elyra turns evil they abandon her and call her a new Belial.

### Series questions

**D12 [DECIDED] CoS genealogy wording (was 23).**
Souls echo through true time. Draicele is itself an experiment about souls, as seen in games 4 and 6 through the mirror world and the completed soul cycle. Written into the hidden gallery scene.

**D13 [AI CALL] Remake vs Baccata's off-screen death (was 24).**
Baccata's death in LoL2 stays canon, because D2 relies on it. Reseeding stays the LoL4 hook. If a remake changes how he dies, he must still end up dead and open to reseeding.

**D14 [AI CALL] GAME3 soul rework vs Copper's Act II voice (was 25).**
Three fixed points hold for Copper: the hostile GAME4 opener, his farewell letter (D8), and a decade of soul research to mend his own damaged soul.

### New content (2026-09-20)

**D15 [DECIDED] Luther's hut.**
A hut where Luther has residence, with magic plants that grow food and so forth.
*AI calls:* it sits on Royal Road South, a day from Gladstone. It is a zero-combat rest stop with a garden, a hearth with the speaking stone, and a workroom. Luther is never present in person, only his voice. The bird roosts there.

**D16 [DECIDED] Mini dungeons.**
Mini dungeons from the time of the Ancients, Dracoid, Huline and Human, with lore and artifacts.
*AI calls:* three sites of four rooms each. The Sundered Observatory (Human, off Royal Road South), the Burrow of Songs (Huline, off the Wither Track), the Sun-Scale Vault (Dracoid, off the Bearing Path, hybrid-blood lock). Each holds lore tablets, one artifact and one golem-release clue. Artifact effects are open.
