## HOW THE THREE GAMES RELATE TO EACH OTHER

### Protagonist structure — deliberately different per game, matches originals
- **LoL1:** one hero (was 4 selectable in the original, now one customizable
  hero with a specialization), grows to a 3-person party
- **LoL2:** solo — Luther, no companions, no command wheel (already locked
  in `MASTER_OVERVIEW.md`, and an exact match to the original)
- **LoL3:** solo — Copper, but with guild-granted familiars as companions,
  plus the four-guild structure governing quests and ability access

### Spell system lineage — one system, not three
- LoL1 keeps its own original 7-spell, charge-based system
- LoL2 introduces the spell-**group** architecture (Spark/Fireball/Prism/
  Summon/Heal/Mist of Doom, each with an Ancient Magic tier) — this becomes
  the trilogy's standard
- LoL3 **reverts to LoL2's spell-group system** rather than carrying its
  own original 14-category system forward. One spell architecture the
  player learns once, in Phase 3, and keeps.

### Art direction — one target across all three
Lands of Lore II is the visual reference point for the whole project (not
just its own game) — full 3D models throughout, no 2D billboard sprites,
art treated as the primary production focus. Locked in all three game
documents identically.

### World geography — quiet continuity easter eggs
- LoL2: a hidden vista in Draracle's Cave shows Gladstone and the White
  Tower visible on the horizon — confirms one continuous world
- LoL1 and LoL3: no location-based geography easter egg locked yet — worth
  deciding whether this pattern continues (a vista, a landmark) or if the
  Asynchronia sightings alone carry that job in those two games

## THE ASYNCHRONIA / CHRONOMANCER THREAD

Full detail lives in `MASTER_STORY_BIBLE_asynchronia_thread.md`. Summary
for this document's purposes: Asynchronia is genuinely alive and present
throughout the CoT era — not a retrofitted cameo. Each game plans for brief,
unexplained sightings of her:

- **LoL1:** hints placed at the White Tower
- **LoL2:** the Draracle's Cave vista (geographic, not a personal sighting)
  — a personal sighting is still an open option for this game
- **LoL3:** she also plays a direct role in the soul-story rework (see
  below) — cryptic guidance, not just a sighting

**Open across all three:** exact number and placement of personal
sightings hasn't been finalized as a single pass. Worth doing once, across
all three games together, rather than per-game — noted as open in every
game document so far.

## THE SOUL STORY (LoL3-specific, but touches the whole mythology)

LoL3's original soul-theft plot is the weak point of the classic trilogy —
a MacGuffin excuse for a five-world fetch quest with no real internal
logic. The proposed fix (detailed in `GAME3_LOL3_CLASSIC_REFERENCE.md`)
connects it directly to the Ancient soul-magic already built for Chronicles
of Souls — the Thomgog soul cycle, Vhalcaris as a corrupted regulator, the
general body of Ancient soul-engineering. **This is flagged, not
confirmed** — it's a real connective decision between the CoT and CoS
mythologies and deserves a deliberate yes/no before it gets written into
anything final.
