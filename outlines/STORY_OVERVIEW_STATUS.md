## LoL5 — SHADOWS OF THE WESTERNLANDS ✅ strong, matches your read

**What's fully built:** the premise, all five principal characters, the complete
three-act structure, both major locations (Gladstone, the Westernlands), all
core gameplay systems (Resonance, Echo Sight, Warden Circle, Dark Army
remnants), and the full Chronowalker throughline.

**What's actually missing (minor):**
- "Supporting Characters" — header only, no names/roles listed
- "Remaining Endings — Overview" — only Ending 1 is written out
- "Series Continuity" — empty
- "Names & Terminology Reference" — empty
- "Agent Structure" / "Development Phases" — empty (these are build-planning
  stubs, not story stubs — lower priority)

**Verdict:** this is your reference standard. The gaps are finishing touches,
not structural holes. Safe to move to a locations pass whenever you want.

## LoL4 — SEEDS OF THE ANCIENTS ✅ good, just fragmented

The story content itself (Thomgog soul cycle, Copper's exile arc, Draicele,
the four expansion zones in `lol4_locations`) is largely complete once you
account for all three files together. The problem isn't missing content —
it's that no single file has all of it, and the two GDD-style versions
predate the Copper/Draicele additions.

**What's missing:** nothing structural. This is a merge job (see the
`PROJECT_ORGANIZATION.md` doc from before), not a writing job.

**Verdict:** ready for a locations pass — arguably it already had one
(`lol4_locations` is exactly that). Once merged, this one's essentially done.

## LoL6 — THE SHINING PATH ⚠️ needs real work before anything else

The document is honest about its own state ("NOT FINAL... the hero's identity
is left open"). The bones are genuinely strong — Valdenmere, the soul cycle
as "love built wrong," the ring/Binding Seal reveal, the Chronowalker
watching her own parents meet — but four things are unresolved, and they're
load-bearing for everything downstream, including Chronicles of Souls.

**The four real gaps (the document names these itself):**
1. **The hero's identity** — completely open. Who are they, why do they have
   the "specific frequency the Path requires"?
2. **The ring's Act 3 function** — "What happens when both halves are in the
   same place is the question Act 3 builds toward" — never answered
3. **The force behind the binding** — something "has been waiting" and
   activates in Act 3, but it's never named or characterized
4. **Mystery One (Scotia's True Nature)** and **Mystery Three (the
   Chronowalker's Father)** — both are section headers with zero content,
   even though Mystery Two (the ring) is fully written

**Also empty:** "Valdenmere's Factions," "Returning Characters," "Valdenmere
Locations — First Pass," and "Open Questions for Development" (which would
normally list exactly these gaps, but is itself blank).

**Verdict:** this needs a dedicated pass before anything else in Chronicles
of Souls can be finished — Chronicles of Souls' own Mystery framework and the
Chronowalker's resolution both assume LoL6's mysteries are locked.

## CHRONICLES OF SOULS ⚠️ skeleton, not a draft

The unifying thesis is genuinely strong and worth protecting: LoL IV, V, and
VI as three different answers to "what is a soul worth," with Draicele as a
hub that exists across all three states at once. That framing document
(section 1, "The Pattern You Have Already Built") is the best-written part
and shouldn't be touched.

But past the framing, most sections are titles with nothing under them:

- Hub Rooms
- Her Relationship with the Era Heroes
- Her Abilities (the connecting hero's)
- Core Cross-Era Soul Effects
- What the Player Finally Understands
- The Four Final Choices (referenced — "None of these choices is wrong" —
  but never listed)
- Recommended Phase Structure / Recommended Agent Structure
- Probability Estimates
- "The One Thing That Makes This Inevitable" (closing section, blank)

**Why it has to wait for LoL6:** the connecting hero's soul-sight ability
explicitly reads "what Valdenmere's binding sealed," and her father's
bloodline is Aldric's — both of those only make sense once LoL6's mysteries
(the ring's function, the villain, the hero) are answered. Writing these
sections now means rewriting them the moment LoL6 changes.

## RECOMMENDED ORDER (matches what you said, made concrete)

1. **LoL6 first.** Resolve the four gaps above — hero, ring mechanic,
   villain, Mysteries One and Three. This is the actual bottleneck.
2. **Chronicles of Souls second.** Its stubs can only be filled honestly
   once LoL6 is locked, since several depend directly on it.
3. **LoL4 merge.** Independent of the above — pure consolidation, do it
   whenever, doesn't block anything.
4. **LoL5 stub-filling.** Also independent, low effort, low urgency.

Once 1 and 2 are solid → locations pass (LoL6 needs one built from scratch;
Chronicles of Souls needs the Hub Rooms designed; LoL4/5 are already
covered) → then the big consolidated reference document.
